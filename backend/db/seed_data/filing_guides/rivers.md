## Tax Authority

The **Rivers State Internal Revenue Service (RIRS)** administers Personal Income Tax for individuals resident in Rivers State, including the Port Harcourt tech and creative-economy hub. RIRS runs a fully digitized revenue platform for Direct Assessment filing.

## Registration & Taxpayer ID (TIN)

Register as an **"Individual"** taxpayer on the RIVTAMIS portal using your **NIN/BVN**, which generates your **RIVTIN (Rivers State Tax Identification Number)**. You need a RIVTIN before you can log in and file.

## How to File Your Direct Assessment Return

The **RIVTAMIS** portal (search "RIVTAMIS" for the current official link — rivtamis.riversbirs.gov.ng at time of writing) handles filing:

1. Log in to RIVTAMIS using your RIVTIN and password.
2. Select **Self-Assessment / New Declaration**.
3. Enter your **gross income breakdown** and **itemized business expense deductions** — use the "Total Income" and itemized "Allowable Deductions" / "Capital Allowances" / "Statutory Reliefs" lists from your GigTax report directly, category by category.
4. Submit your income statement and a bank account summary as supporting documents.
5. RIVTAMIS generates a system assessment bill. Compare it against your GigTax "Net Tax Payable" figure before paying — if there's a large gap, recheck your entries.
6. Pay through online banking or a designated partner bank in Port Harcourt.
7. Declare your payment reference on the portal to instantly generate an e-Receipt, which also triggers TCC processing.

## Filing Deadline

**March 31** annually, for the preceding tax year, consistent with the national norm for state Direct Assessment filing. Confirm on RIVTAMIS each year for any notified extension.

## Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC)

Triggered automatically once your payment reference is declared and confirmed on RIVTAMIS — no separate manual application step.
