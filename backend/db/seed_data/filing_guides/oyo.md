## Tax Authority

The **Oyo State Board of Internal Revenue** (OYSBIR, also referred to on its own materials as OYSIRS) is the state agency responsible for assessing and collecting Personal Income Tax (PIT) from residents of Oyo State, including self-employed individuals, freelancers, and gig workers who are tax resident there under the 183-day / principal-place-of-residence rule. Its public information site is [oyostaterevenue.com](https://oyostaterevenue.com/), and it also maintains a legacy site at [oyostatebir.com](https://www.oyostatebir.com/OyoRevenue/default.aspx).

## Registration & Taxpayer ID (TIN)

OYSBIR issues a **State Tax Identification Number (S-TIN)** to every registered payer, generated through the **Self Service portal at [selfservice.oyostatebir.com](https://selfservice.oyostatebir.com/)**.

- **Individual registration** on the self-service portal asks for: courtesy title, surname, first name, date of birth (must be 18 or older), sex, contact details, residential address, and payer-type classification.
- A separate, more manual registration route also exists: visit [oyostaterevenue.com](https://oyostaterevenue.com/), locate the "Downloads" link, download the relevant registration form, complete it, and submit it to the nearest Tax Office. This is the route the official site itself directs new taxpayers to; it does not spell out whether BVN or NIN is required on the form, so bring both as supporting ID when registering in person.
- Once registered, the S-TIN becomes the login/reference credential for filing returns and generating payment codes on the self-service portal.

*Not fully confirmed: whether online individual registration alone (without an in-person visit) is sufficient to activate a usable S-TIN, or whether OYSBIR still requires the physical form for first-time registrants. Confirm directly with OYSBIR or at a Tax Office.*

## How to File Your Direct Assessment Return

Direct Assessment (Form A) is the route OYSBIR designates for self-employed individuals, distinct from PAYE (which covers salaried employees whose employers remit on their behalf). A GigTax user filing under Direct Assessment should:

1. Register (or confirm existing registration) on the [Self Service portal](https://selfservice.oyostatebir.com/) to obtain an S-TIN, or complete the paper registration form and submit it at the nearest Tax Office if registering manually.
2. Prepare the Form A return using the numbers from your GigTax self-assessment report: total gross income for the year, Section 20 allowable deductions (rent relief, tools of trade, data/utilities, statutory contributions), and the resulting net chargeable income and computed tax payable under the NTA 2025 bands.
3. File the Annual Tax Return with the Assessment Authority nearest to you, either through the Self Service portal (where the portal supports Form A filing online) or by submitting the completed return in person if the assessment officer requires paper submission — OYSBIR's own guidance ("File your Annual Tax Returns with the Assessment Authority nearest to you") does not commit to a fully online-only process, so treat in-person confirmation as the safer default.
4. Receive an **Assessment Notice** from the Assessment Authority showing the tax liability confirmed against your filed return.
5. Pay the assessed amount at a designated bank and obtain a **Payment Slip / deposit teller**.
6. Present the payment slip/teller at your Assessment Authority to receive a **Revenue Receipt**, which is your proof of payment for that assessment year.
7. Alternatively, OYSBIR's self-assessment system allows a new taxpayer to self-assess, pay through a designated bank, and obtain an e-TCC directly, without visiting a tax office — this applies specifically to first-time self-assessment filers per OYSBIR's own published description.

## Filing Deadline

OYSBIR's published material explicitly states an **employer PAYE annual returns deadline of January 31** each year (covering the preceding year's employee emoluments), consistent with Section 81(2) of the Personal Income Tax Act. A specific, explicitly-published deadline date for *self-employed* Direct Assessment filers was not found on OYSBIR's own materials at the time of this research. Under the general PITA framework that also governs Lagos, FCT, and Rivers, self-employed individuals are expected to file within 90 days of the start of the year of assessment, i.e., by **March 31**. Treat March 31 as the working assumption but confirm with OYSBIR directly, since the state site did not state this figure itself.

## Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC)

- A TCC covers the three years of assessment preceding the year of application, so a self-employed applicant needs three years of payment receipts/returns on file.
- **Self-employed applicants:** submit a written application together with the last three years' payment receipts to your Assessment Authority. The Authority reviews the documents and may raise clarifying questions. On approval, you are notified (by SMS/email per the self-service portal) to collect or download your certificate; OYSBIR's self-service portal supports downloading an electronic TCC after login.
- **Employees:** your employer must have filed three years of annual returns and remitted the associated deductions before the Assessment Authority will request issuance of your TCC.
