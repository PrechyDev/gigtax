## Tax Authority

The **Ogun State Internal Revenue Service** (OGIRS) is the state agency responsible for assessing and collecting Personal Income Tax from Ogun-resident taxpayers, including self-employed individuals, freelancers, and gig workers who meet the 183-day / principal-place-of-residence residency test for the state. OGIRS's main office is at Oke-Mosan, Abeokuta.

**A note on portals:** research surfaced more than one Ogun State revenue web presence — [www.ogunstaterevenue.com](https://www.ogunstaterevenue.com/en/selfservice) (self-service section, which itself points to a domain called `taxsmart.ogunstaterevenue.com`), [ogirs.ogunstate.gov.ng](https://ogirs.ogunstate.gov.ng/individual) (an individual registration portal), and [portal.ogetax.ogunstate.gov.ng](https://portal.ogetax.ogunstate.gov.ng/) (branded "OGIRS Portal"). It was not possible to confirm from public sources which of these is the current, canonical live system versus a legacy or parallel one. **Do not assume any single URL above is authoritative** — verify the correct, currently active portal directly with OGIRS (or via www.ogunstaterevenue.com's own navigation) before directing a user to register or file there.

## Registration & Taxpayer ID (TIN)

- OGIRS issues a **State Tax Identification Number (S-TIN)**, used as the login credential on its self-service portal.
- Registration requires creating an account and providing personal details; the individual registration form seen (at ogirs.ogunstate.gov.ng/individual) asks for nationality, and for non-Nigerian applicants, an International Passport or ID upload (PDF, max 5MB), plus selection of the nearest tax station from a list of 30+ locations across the state.
- *Not confirmed*: whether BVN or NIN is explicitly required in the registration form for Nigerian individual applicants — this field was not visible in the page content retrieved, though BVN/NIN-linked verification is standard practice across Nigerian state revenue services and should be expected.
- Downloadable forms for individual registration, corporate registration, and income tax returns are also available from the self-service section of www.ogunstaterevenue.com, for taxpayers who prefer or need a paper-based route.
- If you already hold an S-TIN but have lost it, OGIRS-linked guidance describes an online TIN recovery process through the self-service portal login/password-retrieval flow.

## How to File Your Direct Assessment Return

OGIRS's own site references a specific filing item for "Income Tax Returns for Self Employed and Individual for the Year Ended" the relevant tax year, indicating Direct Assessment filing is treated as its own return category, separate from PAYE. Based on OGIRS's published FAQ and third-party step-by-step guides, a GigTax user filing as self-employed should expect this process:

1. Register for (or confirm) your S-TIN through the correct current OGIRS portal, or submit a paper individual registration form to your nearest OGIRS tax station.
2. Log in to the e-filing/self-service system with your S-TIN and password.
3. Locate the individual/self-employed income tax return form for the relevant year (OGIRS's site lists this as a distinct downloadable/fileable item each year).
4. Enter your income and deduction details using your GigTax self-assessment figures: total gross income for the year, Section 20 allowable deductions (rent relief, tools of trade, data/telecoms, statutory contributions), net chargeable income, and computed tax payable under the NTA 2025 bands.
5. Upload supporting documentation (bank statements, invoices, receipts) substantiating the declared income and claimed deductions.
6. Submit the return through the portal; you should receive an acknowledgment/receipt of submission.
7. Alternatively, OGIRS's published guidance also describes an in-person route: submit annual tax returns to the nearest Assessment Authority, receive an **Assessment Notice** showing the tax due, pay the assessed amount at a designated bank, and present the payment documentation to obtain a **Revenue Receipt**.
8. Where the return is filed online, follow the portal's payment prompts to pay online; where filed in person, pay at a designated bank as above.
9. For continuity, OGIRS's guidance notes that taxpayers who already have tax records at a specific OGIRS office should return to that same office for assessment each year, for consistent record-keeping.

## Filing Deadline

OGIRS's published material states an **employer PAYE annual returns deadline of January 31** each year, covering the preceding year's employee emoluments, with monthly PAYE remittances due by the 10th of the following month. A specific, explicitly-published deadline for *self-employed* Direct Assessment filers was not found on OGIRS's own materials at the time of this research. Under the general PITA framework shared across Nigerian states (also true for Lagos, FCT, and Rivers), self-employed individuals are generally expected to file within 90 days of the start of the assessment year, i.e., by **March 31**. Treat March 31 as the working assumption for Ogun State but confirm directly with OGIRS, since it was not stated explicitly in the state's own self-employed-facing material found.

## Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC)

- OGIRS's e-TCC always covers the **three years of assessment preceding** the year of application, so three years of Direct Assessment payment records are needed.
- **Self-employed applicants:** submit a written application with three years of payment receipts to the relevant Assessment Authority; the Authority reviews the documents, may ask clarifying questions, and on approval, notifies the applicant to collect the certificate.
- **Processing time:** OGIRS's own e-TCC page states certificates are issued **72 hours after payment and proper filing of all required documents**.
- **Cost:** OGIRS's e-TCC page explicitly states there is **no processing fee** for the certificate.
