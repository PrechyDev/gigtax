Great question about your new laptop. Under Nigerian tax regulations for freelancers (sole proprietors), laptops are considered Capital Allowances rather than immediate deductible expenses.

  • Initial Allowance: You can claim a percentage (typically 50% for plant/machinery/equipment) in the year of purchase.
  • Annual Allowance: The remaining value is depreciated over subsequent years (typically 25% annually).
